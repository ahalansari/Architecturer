## Not professional advice

Plans, 3D models, renders, validation results and every engineering output (structure and construction build-ups,
air conditioning loads and duct sizes, water and drainage hydraulics, pump pits, electrical layouts and circuits,
lifts, lighting levels, airflow studies, walkability checks, irrigation) are **indicative**. They are produced by
simplified models and rules of thumb, and can be wrong or incomplete.

They are **not** a substitute for the work of licensed architects, engineers and other qualified professionals. Before
anything is built, every design must be checked, completed and signed off by people qualified to do so, against the
building codes, fire and life-safety regulations, accessibility rules and standards that apply where it will be built,
and approved by the authorities having jurisdiction. A plan that passes `validate` is not code-compliant by that fact.

## Healthcare buildings and medical equipment

- The medical equipment models (hospital beds, monitors, operating tables, surgical lights, CT / MRI / X-ray, laboratory
  equipment and the rest) are **generic, representative shapes** for space planning and visualisation. They do not
  represent any manufacturer's product, its dimensions, weight, service needs or performance, and are not intended for
  any clinical, diagnostic or medical-device purpose.
- Healthcare facilities are governed by specialised requirements (for example national health ministry guidelines,
  the FGI Guidelines, HTM / HBN guidance, or whatever applies locally): infection control, ventilation rates and
  pressure regimes, medical gases, nurse call, radiation shielding, MRI safety zoning, structural floor loading for
  imaging equipment, emergency power, fire compartmentation and evacuation of patients. Architecturer does **not**
  design or verify these. Equipment rooms must be set out from the chosen vendor's site-planning documentation.
- Healthcare heat gains, room-size hints and clearances in the guide are starting points only.

## Costs, quantities and contracts

Cost estimates, rates, allowances (including medical equipment allowances), bills of quantities and shopping lists are
indicative and may be out of date; get quotations. Generated works contracts are templates, not legal advice: have them
reviewed by a lawyer qualified in the relevant jurisdiction before anyone signs them.

## Large projects

Very large plans (hundreds of thousands to millions of m²) are supported through level-scoped checks, massing views and
per-level exports. Speed and memory use depend on the hardware; level-scoped validation does not replace validating the
whole plan.
